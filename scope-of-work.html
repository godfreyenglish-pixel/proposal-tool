Battery Scope of Work
Your Battery Scope of Work
A battery is only as good as the plan around it. Here is the full process we run so your system is sized right, installed clean, switched onto the right rate, and earning its keep every single day. No surprises, no gaps.
The process
I
Consultation & Energy Analysis
We pull your Con Edison usage (Green Button interval data where available), map how and when you use power, and model your real savings before you spend a dollar. You see the numbers first.
II
Custom Proposal & Plan
We size the battery to your home, build the time-of-use rate strategy that makes the switch pay, and line up every incentive you qualify for (like the NYSERDA storage incentive). A clear plan and clear numbers.
III
Professional Site Survey
A technician visits, assesses your electrical panel, service capacity, and the loads you want backed up, and confirms the real-world design for your home. This is where the plan gets locked to your actual house.
IV
Engineering, Permits & Interconnection
We produce the stamped system design, pull the electrical and building permits, file your Con Edison interconnection application, and reserve your NYSERDA incentive. All the paperwork is ours to carry.
V
Professional Installation
Our licensed crew installs the battery, inverter, and any critical-load subpanel, wires it to code, and leaves the job site clean. Most residential installs are done in one to two days.
VI
Commissioning & Rate Switch
We test the system end to end, set up your monitoring, program the battery to charge overnight and cover the expensive peak window, and get you enrolled on the time-of-use rate. This is the step that turns hardware into savings.
VII
Monitoring & Ongoing Support
We keep an eye on performance so your battery keeps dispatching at the right times as rates and seasons change. If something drifts, we catch it. A system that keeps earning, not one you have to babysit.
