Rivertown Solar Battery Warranty
Your Battery Is Covered
Three layers of protection stand behind your system: the equipment, our installation, and its performance over time. Here is exactly what that means — with the real terms for the batteries we install.
Layer 1 — The Equipment
Manufacturer warranty on your battery
We install two of the most trusted batteries on the market. Here are the actual warranty terms for each — your system uses one of them.
Enphase IQ Battery 10C 15-Year Warranty
Covered for 15 years (or 6,000 charge cycles, whichever comes first). Enphase also guarantees the battery's capacity: it will still hold at least 70% at year 10 and at least 60% at year 15. That's a written promise on how much energy it stores as it ages — not just protection against outright failure. 10 kWh usable, ~7 kW output.
EG4 PowerPro WallMount 10-Year Warranty
Covered for 10 years — full replacement in year one, then prorated coverage through year ten. Built rugged for all-weather install (IP65, self-heating) with a 14.3 kWh usable capacity and an engineering design life rated beyond 8,000 cycles. We register your warranty for you at commissioning so your coverage starts clean.
Not sure which one is in your proposal? Just ask — we'll point to yours.
Layer 2 — Our Work
Rivertown Workmanship — 10 Years
Our own 10-year workmanship guarantee covers the labor and the quality of the install — the wiring, the mounting, the connections, the panel work. If anything we installed needs attention because of how it was installed, we fix it at no cost to you.
Layer 3 — Performance Over Time
It keeps its strength
Batteries hold slightly less charge as they age — that's normal. With Enphase, the exact retention is guaranteed in writing (70% at year 10, 60% at year 15). EG4 is engineered for a long service life and its 10-year warranty covers defects and premature failure. Either way, you're protected — and we're monitoring it the whole time.
We watch it so you don't have to.
Your system is monitored. If performance drifts or a fault shows up, we often see it before you do — and we start the warranty claim for you. You're never left chasing a manufacturer on your own.
